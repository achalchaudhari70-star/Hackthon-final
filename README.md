# 🩺 AI HEALTH CARE ASSISTANT

### 🤖 *An AI-Powered Multi-Model, Multi-Disease Prediction System*

> **Predict Early • Stay Aware • Build a Healthier Future 💙**

Welcome to the final hackathon project by **TECH TITANS**! 🚀

**AI Health Care Assistant** is a smart healthcare application that uses **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** to provide preliminary predictions for multiple health conditions — all in **one simple and user-friendly platform**.

Instead of using separate systems for different diseases, our application brings together multiple trained models to make health-risk prediction **simple, fast, and accessible**.

## ✨ What Can Our AI Health Care Assistant Do?

### 🫀 Heart Disease Prediction

Analyzes health-related parameters and predicts the possible risk of heart disease.

### 🫘 Kidney Disease Prediction

Uses a trained Machine Learning model to predict the possible risk of kidney disease.

### 🩺 Common Illness Prediction

Helps predict possible common illnesses based on the user's input.

### 💡 Smart Health Guidance

Provides short and simple guidance based on the prediction result.

### 💬 AI Health Chatbot

Allows users to ask general health-related questions and receive helpful responses.

### 🖥️ Simple & Interactive Interface

Designed to make the application easy and convenient to use.
